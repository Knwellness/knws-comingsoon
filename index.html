About Me

Hi, I'm Kayla, a massage therapist based in Doncaster. I specialise in sports, relaxation, and hot stone massage, working with clients who experience muscle tension, stress, stiffness, or general discomfort from everyday life or physical activity.

My approach is focused and intuitive — I take time to understand what your body needs on the day and adapt each treatment accordingly. No two sessions are the same.

I'm fascinated by how the body holds tension and how massage can restore movement, reduce pain, and support both physical and mental wellbeing. My goal is to create a calm, comfortable space where you feel listened to and properly looked after.

Appointments are one-to-one and fully tailored, allowing you to switch off and get the most from your treatment.

A Few Questions You Might Have

Everything you need to know before your appointment.

What can I expect at my first appointment?

When you arrive, we'll have a little chat about what you're looking for from your massage, any areas of tension or discomfort, and your preferred pressure. This helps me tailor your treatment to you.

I'll explain everything before we begin, and you'll have privacy to get ready. Throughout your treatment, you'll remain appropriately covered, and I'll always make sure you're comfortable.

There's no need to feel nervous or know exactly what to expect. I'll guide you through everything so you can relax and enjoy your time.

Where is the studio?

KN Wellness Studio is a private garden studio in New Rossington, Doncaster. Full directions will be provided when your appointment is confirmed.

Is there parking available?

Please contact me before your appointment for information about parking and arrival.

What should I wear?

Wear something comfortable. You'll be given privacy to get ready, and you'll remain appropriately covered throughout your massage.

Do I need to bring anything?

No, everything needed for your treatment is provided. Just arrive ready to relax.

Can I choose the massage pressure?

Absolutely. Your treatment is tailored to you, and you can ask for lighter or firmer pressure at any time.

What if I've never had a massage before?

That's absolutely fine. I'll talk you through everything beforehand and make sure you feel comfortable.

Health & Wellbeing

Your wellbeing is my priority. Please do not book an appointment if you are currently experiencing any serious or contagious medical conditions, or if you have recently undergone surgery, unless you have received clearance from your GP or healthcare provider.

If you have any injuries, medical conditions, or are pregnant, please let me know before your appointment so your treatment can be safely adapted to your individual needs.

Massage is a complementary therapy and does not replace medical advice or treatment.
