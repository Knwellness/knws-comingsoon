Treatments

Relaxation Massage

Slow, flowing techniques to calm the nervous system, reduce stress, and release full-body tension.

Choose this if: you feel stressed, overwhelmed, struggle to switch off, or want full relaxation.

Sports Massage

Deep tissue work targeting tight muscles, improving mobility, and supporting recovery and pain relief.

Choose this if: you have tight muscles, train regularly, sit for long hours, or are dealing with pain or injury.

Hot Stone Massage

Heated stones melt deep muscle tension, improve circulation, and create full-body relaxation.

Choose this if: you want deep relaxation, feel physically tense, or prefer a soothing heat-based treatment.
