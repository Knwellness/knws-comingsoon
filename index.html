Frequently Asked Questions

A few things you might like to know before visiting KN Wellness Studio.

What can I expect at my first appointment?

When you arrive, we'll have a little chat about what you're looking for from your massage, any areas of tension or discomfort, and your preferred pressure. This helps me tailor your treatment to you.

I'll explain everything before we begin, and you'll have privacy to get ready. Throughout your treatment, you'll remain appropriately covered, and I'll always make sure you're comfortable.

There's no need to feel nervous or know exactly what to expect. I'll guide you through everything so you can relax and enjoy your time.

Where is the studio located?

KN Wellness Studio is a private garden studio in New Rossington, Doncaster, on the Heatherfields estate, just behind Lidl.

Full directions and the address will be provided when your appointment is confirmed.

Is parking available?

On-street parking is available on the roads around and outside the property.

When you arrive, please use the gate down the side of the house. You'll see a sign on the garage directing you. Once through the gate, follow the path straight ahead to the garden studio, where I'll be waiting to welcome you.

What should I wear or bring to my appointment?

There's no need to bring anything with you. Just wear whatever you're comfortable travelling in.

Towels and everything needed for your treatment will be provided, and you'll have privacy to get ready when you arrive.

Can I choose the massage pressure?

Absolutely. Your treatment is tailored to you, and you can ask for lighter or firmer pressure at any time. Your comfort is always important, so please let me know how you're feeling throughout your appointment.

What if I've never had a massage before?

That's absolutely fine! I'll talk you through everything beforehand and make sure you feel comfortable throughout your treatment. You don't need any previous experience.

How often should I have a massage?

This depends on your individual needs, lifestyle and personal preferences. Some people enjoy an occasional treatment to unwind, while others prefer to make massage part of their regular self-care routine.

We can discuss what works best for you during your appointment.

How do I pay?

I accept cash, bank transfers and card payments, including contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book directly using the online booking system below. Simply choose your preferred treatment and an available appointment time.

If you have any questions or need help choosing a treatment, feel free to contact me on WhatsApp.

Health & Wellbeing

Your wellbeing is my priority. Please let me know about any medical conditions, injuries, recent surgery or other health concerns before your appointment so we can discuss whether massage is suitable and whether any adaptations are needed.

If you are pregnant or have an existing medical condition, please contact me before booking so we can discuss the most appropriate treatment.

If you are experiencing a serious or contagious medical condition, please postpone your appointment until it is safe to attend. Where appropriate, please seek advice or clearance from your GP or healthcare provider.

Massage is a complementary therapy and does not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
