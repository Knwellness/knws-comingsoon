About Me

Hi, I’m Kayla, a qualified massage therapist based in Doncaster, working from a private garden studio. I specialise in sports, relaxation, and hot stone massage, helping clients reduce muscle tension, ease stress, and support recovery.

Each treatment is tailored to your individual needs on the day, whether you’re dealing with stiffness from work, training, or general tension. My focus is on providing a calm, one-to-one environment where you can fully relax and feel at ease.

All appointments take place in a quiet private studio setting, away from busy clinics, allowing for a more personal and focused treatment experience.
