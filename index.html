About Me

Hi, I’m Kayla, a massage therapist based in Doncaster. I specialise in sports, relaxation, and hot stone massage, working with clients who experience muscle tension, stress, stiffness, or general discomfort from everyday life or physical activity.

My approach is focused and intuitive — I take time to understand what your body needs on the day and adapt each treatment accordingly. No two sessions are the same.

I’m fascinated by how the body holds tension and how massage can restore movement, reduce pain, and support both physical and mental wellbeing. My goal is to create a calm, comfortable space where you feel listened to and properly looked after.

Appointments are one‑to‑one and fully tailored, allowing you to switch off and get the most from your treatment.

  • ✔ ITEC Qualified Massage Therapist
  • ✔ Fully insured
  • ✔ Client comfort and communication always prioritised
